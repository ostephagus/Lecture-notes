This course follows on directly from IB Quantum Mechanics. We will:
\begin{itemize}
    \item reformulate QM in a more powerful, flexible and useful form that goes beyond wavefunctions -- the Dirac formalism;
    \item thereby perform much simpler analysis of known problems like the quantum harmonic oscillator;
    \item discuss new concepts such as spin, symmetries (with their conserved quantities), and how to define whether particles are identical;
    \item provide a framework for quantising more general systems like the electromagnetic field;
    \item introduce perturbation theory as a practical tool for computations.
\end{itemize}
